About

I've spent my career in product marketing. I figure out how a product works, figure out what users care about and then connect the two, explaining the product or feature, launching it, and getting users to actually use it (the hardest part if you ask me).

I started building with AI at Instawork. I built lifecycle SMS agents, a handful of internal agents, and a Replit app for our GTM team. It was some of the most fun I had at work, and I kept asking the question every good product marketer asks: how does this actually work?

So I'm taking a few months to find out. I'm building with AI every day, mostly in Claude Code. I'm not a trained engineer, so my job is deciding what to build, how it should behave, and whether it's actually right.

A few things I've learned about how I work:

  • Almost every project so far has had a moment where I push back on something AI decided on its own, and each catch becomes a rule it follows from then on.
  • My projects are built from what I know. Nearly every rule in my book fair advisor came from knowing kids and books, not from anything technical.
  • I want to see the actual results, not just a summary. When AI wrote both the test emails and the answer key for a project and told me everything passed, I had questions.
  • The parts I love are the judgment calls about how a product should behave. The parts I don't are hosting and figuring out databases, but can you blame me?

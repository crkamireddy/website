About

I've spent my career in product marketing. I figure out how a product works, explain it so people care, launch it, and get them to actually use it (the hardest part if you ask me).

At Instawork, I started building with AI. I built lifecycle SMS agents, a handful of internal agents, and a Replit app for our account managers. It was the most fun I had at work, and I kept asking the question every good product marketer asks: how does this actually work?

So I'm taking a few months to see how far I can go. I'm building with AI every day, mostly in Claude Code. I'm not a trained engineer. I direct, I review, and I make myself read the code it writes. See what I'm working on under Projects.
